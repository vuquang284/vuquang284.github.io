Autonomous Aircraft Development for RoboNation SUAS

I led technical development efforts for Maryland Unmanned Aerial Systems (MUAS), a student engineering team designing and developing autonomous aircraft for the RoboNation Student Unmanned Aerial Systems (SUAS) Competition.

My responsibilities included coordinating development across mechanical, electrical, and software subteams, contributing to airframe design and mechanical integration, and supporting payload integration, sensor selection, technical documentation, and flight testing.

UMD xFoundry Crossfire | Aug 2025 – May 2026

Autonomous Drone Development for Wildfire Response

As part of the Crossfire team, I worked on developing an autonomous wildfire suppression system using the Freefly Alta X drone. My primary responsibility was developing the autonomous mission logic using ROS 2 and PX4, allowing the suppression drone to receive target coordinates from a separate fire detection drone, autonomously navigate to identified fire locations, and execute payload deployment procedures.

I also utilized PX4 Software-in-the-Loop (SITL) simulation to develop, test, and refine autonomous flight behaviors and mission sequences before deployment on physical hardware. This work provided hands-on experience with UAV autonomy, ROS 2 integration, flight control systems, and simulation-based validation.
